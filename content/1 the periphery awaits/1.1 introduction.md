## the periphery
civilisation thins out the further you get from earth. the *sector* of densely populated planets that have centralised governments is called the [[1.3 places to be#old.orbit|old.orbit]]. after that there's the [[1.3 places to be#inner beltway|inner beltway]], a looser stretch of corporate-controlled systems. past that there's the [[1.3 places to be#outer beltway|outer beltway]], an interarm gap, **a dead zone spanning thousands of light-years**; barren of any star or planet, not even light could penetrate its veil. humanity thought that was the end of space. they were wrong.

something possessed [[Meridian Co]] to look into the outer beltway and they found a speck of light beyond the veil. they sent an expedition with enough food and energy to last a century and aimed for it. 172 years later, confirmation came back. there was something beyond the veil. Meridian coined the name [[1.3 places to be#periphery|periphery]] and discovered **it is rich in every resource humanity yearned for**. it also harboured [[1.5 the known unknowns#anomalies|anomalies]] creeping from the dark.

not long after the discovery Meridian called a meeting through the *megacorp* mediator [[k.holdings]] to propose the *orion accord*, **dividing the periphery into five zones**, one for each *megacorp*:
1. [[Meridian Co]] - [[1.3 places to be#corvane landing|corvane landing]]
2. [[Kovacs-Oyabu]] - [[1.3 places to be#greyharbour|greyharbour]]
3. [[Mithras]] - [[1.3 places to be#halyard reach|halyard reach]]
4. [[Dept. 7]] - [[1.3 places to be#tarpit junction|tarpit junction]]
5. [[TAIYANG H.I.]] - [[1.3 places to be#farside|farside]]

Meridian with its knowledge of launchpads started construction of the [[1.3 places to be#connector nodes|connector nodes]], four launchpads stitched together so the crossing was possible in days. Meridian tried **operating its zone directly**, but learned the hard way that **it was too costly**: too many hazard payouts, too many anomalies disrupting operations, too many leeches stealing company secrets. 

[[k.holdings]] called for a *megacorp* meeting and proposed the *oversight program*. the plan was to morph the periphery into a contractor economy, distancing the *megacorp* influence from the periphery so a [[PCOC|commission]] could **foster a highly competitive market**, while the *megacorps* held back and taxed everything coming in and out. another bonus of this scheme was the massive quantities of data harvested by PCOC. every mineral, every anomaly, every person of interest is stored in databases shared with the *megacorps.*

> [!info] tldr 
> - the periphery is far away from the populated centre
> - it is divided into five zones
> - the zones are too far apart to travel between
> - getting there requires a connector node; getting back is never guaranteed

## a beacon of resources
over the centuries the periphery has become the **prime exporter of natural resources**. companies could extract some of the same resources from the old.orbit and the beltway **but not in the same quantities without doing irreparable damage to their own worlds**. over time the periphery has become the place with the know-how to extract and refine these resources on the industrial scale that the old.orbit demands. 

a curious phenomenon is noted: the more extraction takes place the more frequent anomaly sightings become. **in some cases previously quiet beltway zones are now reporting anomaly sightings**.

> [!info] tldr 
> - the periphery is rich in resources
> - the other *sectors* depend on its resource extraction
> - extraction makes anomaly sightings more frequent

## the present
today, the periphery is a booming economic centre. contractors from all over the beltway, even specialised professionals from the old.orbit, venture to the periphery for work. most never stay permanently, it's a temporary, deadly, lucrative job for the bravest or greediest souls in the galaxy. **the most desperate people dream of moving to the periphery for some years through an indenture contract or a one-way ticket and work until one job pays enough to retire**.

the *megacorps* don't directly operate in the periphery; they let the tier-twos (*mesos*) and tier-threes (*micros*) do the extracting and rudimentary refinement. from there, the resources get transported to the inner beltway where they are manufactured for low-value goods or shipped to the old.orbit where the *megacorp* factories are. **the only requirement is that they follow PCOC's licence agreement**, if they don't, they are banned from using the connector nodes and embargoed by the *megacorps*.

a new type of corporation has been created in the periphery. the *charters* are quick response teams authorised by PCOC; they **function as the emergency services in the periphery**.

> [!info] tldr 
> - the periphery is an escape from poverty so it attracts loads of low-skill labour
> - the *megacorps* don't operate directly in the periphery, they let the *mesos* and *micros* do the heavy work
> - PCOC has employed *charters* to do basic services in the periphery
