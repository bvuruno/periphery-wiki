## timeline
### late 3000s
- humanity settled its first planet
### late 4000s
- [[Meridian Co]] pioneers the launchpad
### mid 6000s
- after constant tensions with the empires and federations of the [[1.3 places to be#old.orbit|old.orbit]], the then four megacorps established the [[1.3 places to be#beltway|beltway]] *sector*
### mid 7000s
- Meridian sends out the *corvane expedition* to explore the outer beltway. 172 years later, Meridian received a single message from the void: "novus orbis"
- [[TAIYANG H.I.]] rises to become the fifth megacorp
### 8010
- Meridian calls for the *orion accord*, dividing up the periphery into five zones
### 8131
- Meridian constructed the first connector node and tried to assert direct control of its zone: [[1.3 places to be#corvane landing|corvane landing]]
### 8278
- [[k.holdings]] calls for a megacorp meeting to propose the *oversight program*, distancing the megacorps from the periphery and creating [[PCOC]] (the periphery corporate oversight commission)
### 8368
- second connector node finished construction: [[Kovacs-Oyabu]] - [[1.3 places to be#greyharbour|greyharbour]]
### 8579
- third connector node finished construction: [[Mithras]] - [[1.3 places to be#halyard reach|halyard reach]]
### 8766
- fourth connector node finished construction: [[Dept. 7]] - [[1.3 places to be#tarpit junction|tarpit junction]]
### 8888
- fifth connector node finished construction: [[TAIYANG H.I.]] - [[1.3 places to be#farside|farside]]
### 9123
- present day
