## sectors
a *sector* is a rough portion of the galaxy. there is no real marker where a *sector* starts and stops. this leads to constant tension between the old.orbit and the beltway of where jurisdiction begins and ends.
![[milky way.webp]]
### old.orbit
it sits at the core of the galaxy, dense with old empires, federations, republics, even the odd socialist state, the lucky firsts worlders who never had to leave the comfort of established law. the number of permanently inhabited worlds is in the mid hundreds. 
even though the *megacorps* fought tooth and nail for the old empires to recognise the beltway they never moved their headquarters there. to this day the five *megacorps* have old.orbit addresses.
to reach the beltway from the centre, 3 jumps are needed.
### beltway
after the beltway was established *mesos* flocked to it to carve out their own markets not to be crushed under the stranglehold the megacorps had in the old.orbit.
#### inner beltway
the habitable part of the beltway home of *mesos*, caudillos and cheap factories.
to reach the outer beltway, it takes 8-9 jumps.
#### outer beltway
the dark veil that surrounds the galaxy. (~3k technicians via the connector nodes)
to reach the periphery, it takes 4 jumps in the connector node.

## launchpads
interstellar travel was never easy or fast. **ships depend on launch pads**, massive space stations that shoot entire ships across *sectors*. information travels by the same system as everything else. they are tremendously expensive to build and maintain, whole cities have grown up around the busiest pads. 
travel within and between the old.orbit and the beltway is simple enough, just like a metro station or a bus stop.
within the old.orbit [[k.holdings]] established a neutral transport authority, the [[UTA]], to avoid conflict between the *megacorps*. in the beltway, *megacorps* and *mesos* operate mixed pads.
### connector nodes
travelling to the periphery is more complicated. the beltway and the periphery are connected by connector nodes. four launchpads **chained together** to cover the distance needed to reach the periphery. at the ends there are connector stations; most of the periphery population lives around them.
### communications
information travels by the same **launch pad system** as everything else, messages hop **from pad to pad, about 10 hours each**.
- old.orbit --> beltway: news arrives the same day
- beltway --> periphery: about 5 days
- local periphery --> outpost: 3-6h
some packets get lost in transit; some arrive corrupted. some stations forward everything they receive. others filter, delay, or sell access.
