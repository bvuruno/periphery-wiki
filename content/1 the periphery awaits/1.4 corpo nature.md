- [[PCOC]] pseudo-government for the periphery
- [[UTA]] sole launchpad operator in the old.orbit
## corps
the suits and ties, the executives, the entities known as corporations operate a tiered pecking order
1. diversified conglomerates (*megacorps* - old.orbit)
2. market leaders (*mesos* - beltway)
3. local firms (*micros* - periphery)
each pushes into the next tier down to gain **market dominance**.
### tier 1s MEGACORPS (megacorps)
all the megacorps have these key industries but each one has its own edge:
1. manufacturing & heavy industry
2. resource extraction & refinement
3. financial services
4. energy generation & gravity labs
5. colonial development & terraforming
6. security & internal control
7. expeditionary forces & asset recovery
8. media & communications
9. cognition department (ai lab)
10. biotechnology & synthetic integration (androids)
11. transportation, shipyards & orbital yards 
    (they all take turns making launchpads for the [[UTA]])
