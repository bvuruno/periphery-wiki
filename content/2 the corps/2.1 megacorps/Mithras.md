# Mithras Foundries Pty LLC
specialists in salvage, reclamation, and "asset recovery". mithras technicians are legendary for walking into radiation-soaked wrecks and walking out with functional fusion cores. they are pioneers in propulsion tech and cryogenics. their communication logistics arm runs a good chunk of the relay network that ferry news between zones, which means mithras often knows a disaster happened before anyone sends out the call for help.
