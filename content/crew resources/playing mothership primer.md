# opener
hello, welcome to **the Periphery**, a mothership "west marches"[^1] campaign where youll work for a company doing odd jobs in space 
- assisting in scavenging operations
- exploring the frontier
- investigating anomalies
- getting paid

## things to consider 
this campaign is made for the purpose to have fun! so consider the following before joining a session
- **character death is common** 
  *(i will warn you when youre in danger and able to one-shotted but be aware of the fragility of the pc)*
- **violence is punishing**
  *(violence is deadly and should be avoided at all costs, i wont design an encounter with a fight in mind)*
- **the odds to be stacked against you**
  *(rolls are punishing, always try to find a creative way to do something and i will reward you)*
- **you'll make difficult choices**
  *(surviving, solving a mystery, or saving the day are often mutually exclusive)*
- **try to pay attention**
  *(it helps you and everyone else get into the spirit of the game and have a more immersive experience)*

## how to have a good time
- *dont* force yourself to attend to every session, if youre not feeling it dont worry
- *dont* force yourself to read every message or document, play when you want to avoid burnout
- *dont* be scared to post the first message, the west marches formula thrives on pc communication and community

# system overview
hey, if youre coming from D&D mothership is going to feel different, here's the quick system rundown
##### vibe shift  
mothership is a **horror survival game**, not a heroic fantasy game. the goal isnt to win fights, its to *survive, get paid, and get home*
##### rolls work backward from what youre used to  
you have 4 stats: **strength, speed, intellect, combat**
roll **UNDER** your stat on a d100 to succeed, crits on double digits. high stat = good, but youre still rolling percentile dice so failure is always a real possibility even at high stats
##### you roll way less than in dnd  
the warden calls for a **roll only when failure would be costly**. a lot of the game is just talking, deciding, describing what you do. when the dice do come out, it usually means something is about to happen
##### no classes, no levels, 4 roles instead
- **marine** — combat and armor, the tank
- **android** — hacking and androids, the tech specialist
- **scientist** — biology, chemistry, hacking, the science generalist
- **teamster** — piloting, mechanical repair, the "keeps the ship alive" guy

you **dont level up by killing monsters**, you (rarely) *level up by surviving* traumatic stuff and spending downtime training
##### combat is short and lethal, avoid it  
theres **no expectation youll win a fight**, two solid hits can kill a character. *the system wants you to talk*, run, hide, or get creative before you draw a weapon
##### stress and panic instead of hit points 
alongside health, **you track stress**. high stress forces panic checks, bad panic results range from "mildly inconvenient" to "actively work against your crew."
##### gear is scarce and matters a lot  
no bag of infinite holding, inventory slots are limited, ammo is limited, **a flashlight or a rope can be as clutch as a weapon**
## tldr for d&d players
- youre fragile, act like it
- combat is a last resort, not the main event
- theres no "correct" build, roleplay and problem solving carry more weight than character sheet optimisation
- your character isnt destined for greatness, theyre a worker trying to make it to payday

if you want the deeper mechanical rundown (rolling, combat math, panic table) lmk and i can point you to the full rules doc, this is just enough to not be lost at the table

# faqs
## WELCOME TO REAC *(New Crew Orientation)* 

### CHARACTER CREATION / CREW-JOINING RULES 
- **how does a new player create a crew member?** 
  if you have mothership experience there is a google doc in the bottom of the channel, add a new tab and write up crew member, but if youre new to the system or just nervous in general dm me or say in the schedule channel that youre new so i can help you 10-15 minutes before the session 
- **is there a roster of pre-made pcs we can adopt?** 
  no, full custom creation (please 2 sentence backstories at max, no backstory preferred) 
- **if my character dies mid-session, can i make a new one right away, or do i sit out?**
  youll have to wait until the crew is out of danger and youll rejoin as someone else

### MONEY, GEAR & JOBS 
- **do we get paid mid-job or only on completion/return to pad?** 
  on return, job payout gets split into 50% to crew / 10% to ledger / 40% to the charter
- **what happens to payout if the crew fails the job or has to bail?** 
  if its a normal job, not much, someone else will take it if its a contract, you wont get paid and the contractor will remember that 
- **what happens if we don't finish a job in one session, does it carry over, or does each session wrap cleanly?**
  when the time is up youll have to return to the ship
- **can we buy/upgrade gear between sessions?** 
  yes, there is a play by post channel for this, if youre not able to make it to sessions 
- **is there a death/injury payout or insurance, given how dangerous jobs are?** 
  small injuries will be paid by REAC, more severe ones, out of pocket
