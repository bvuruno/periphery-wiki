deep in the granules of space, far away from empires and megacorps there is the **periphery**, the frontier of space exploration. **micros** (micro-corps) inhabit this zone to mine and settle new planets and asteroids, and when things go wrong they call the **auxiliary charters** for help, small crews who risk their lives to do odd jobs in space.
youll be joining [[REAC]] the *reconnaissance and engineering auxiliary charter*

## where are we?
it is the **eighth millennium** (80XX). civilisation thins out the further you get from the center, the number of permanently inhabited worlds could be counted in the low hundreds:
- ***the old.orbit*** sits at the core, dense with old empires, federations, and republics, the comfort of **established law** (~49b pop.)
- ***the beltway*** lies beyond, a looser stretch of **corporate controlled planets and warlords**, where the rule of law starts to bend to whoever's paying (~11b pop.)
- ***the periphery*** is past that, the frontier, it is divided into **6 subzones** with their respective connector pads. the closest thing we have to government here is the [[PCOC]], the periphery oversight commission, they control the connector pads and levy fines to unregistered operations (~9m pop.)
- ***the dim*** is the furthest you can get, its dark, unmapped and unstable. almost completely void of life, **the true dark where help stops answering** (data not gathered)

## the pads?
interstellar travel has never been easy or fast. **ships depend on launch pads**, massive space stations that shoot entire ships across the void, **chained together to move across zones**. 
information travels by the same system as everything else. 
they are tremendously expensive to build and maintain, whole cities have grown up around the busiest pads. 
the beltway and the periphery are connected by 6 connector nodes, built one at a time, managed by PCOC. the connector nodes are too far apart to travel between them and each node has its respective subzone.
- cn1 Kameiro City - meridian's rest (~2.5m pop.)
- cn2 Ashcroft Port - corvane landing (~2m pop.)
- cn3 Appolyon Station - greyharbour (~500k pop.)
- cn4 Redwater Harbour - halyard reach (~1.5m pop.)
- cn5 La Punta - tarpit junction (~1.2m pop.)
- cn6 Westwick Point - farside (~650k pop.)

## REAC?
its one of many charters in the periphery, youll join one of the divisions under a [[kesslers|kessler]]. youll choose jobs from the OPERATIONS TERMINAL[^1], seek out long term contracts with the corps, explore the periphery, investigate intercepted comms/rumors and catalogue anomalies
### do we get paid?
as a worker for a charter you get a monthly salary[^2]:
- marine 250cr/mo
- android 250cr/mo
- scientist 450cr/mo
- teamster 300cr/mo

jobs get posted in the OPERATIONS TERMINAL by color:
- **green.** regular jobs, minicorps *(1,000-4,000cr)*
- **yellow.** hazards included, minicorps *(4,000-8,000cr)*
- **red.** needs connections, corps *(8,000-15,000cr)*
- **black.** high-rep only, corps  *(15,000-30,000cr)*
- **gold.** megacorp job *(???)*

as a registered PCOC chater we are obligated to distress signals *(we are not obligated to take any work pro-bono but the charter maintains as an ESG principal to take ~50% of distress calls pro-bono)*

## the corps?
the suit and ties, the executives, the entities known as corporations have their [[corps|own tiered pecking order]]
1. diversified conglomerates (megacorps - old.orbit)
2. sector leaders (corps - beltway)
3. established firms (micros/micro-corps - periphery)
each one controls their territory and push into the latter to gain *market dominance*

## what else is out there?
what we encounter and cannot explain we categorise and file as **"anomalies"**, it is common knowledge they exist but we are not sure of their nature or reason. the most accepted theory is that they are attracted to activity. 
we divide them into (from **least to most dangerous**):
- t class: tech, unexplained inconsistencies and/or signal deterioration 
- s class: structural/spac inconsistencies
- p class: predators, hunts humans or livestock 
- c class: cognitive

**field procedure** encountering an anomaly follows:
1. do not engage
2. confirm it's actually reportable
3. identify the class
4. route through your crew manager
5. don't file if you're not sure it happened
reports to PCOC are mandatory, youll gain a small bonus per of the detail of the report.
