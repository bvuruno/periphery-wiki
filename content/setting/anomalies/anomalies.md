**nobody calls them "aliens"** 
that word implies a species, a homeworld, something with an intent you could negotiate with, the working term is **anomalies**. 
there is no shared origin, no single species, no motive. whatever gets filed under "anomaly" is united by one thing: *it doesn't make sense, and it usually doesn't end well for whoever found it.*
it's not that anomalies were pushed outward by human activity and now sit quietly in the dark skulking. 
it's the opposite, **activity is the beacon.** every corp that profits from pushing further out is, whether they admit it or not, the reason the anomaly problem exists at all. every terraformed planet, every signal, every jump, **it keeps calling them.**
## classification
classification is **by behaviour**, the categories are a convenience, not a taxonomy. (from least to most dangerous)
### t class - tech
touches signal, data, memory, comms. a relay station running faster than physics should allow, nobody wants to ask why, because the answer might mean turning it off. the most commercially exploited class and the most quietly dangerous, because someone is always tempted to keep using it rather than report it
### s class - structural/spacial
doesn't act, exists wrong. a room bigger inside than on the outside or even worse a hull that's bigger on the outside than the inside. doors that open onto the wrong side of the ship. a section of space where time moves slightly slower. no hostility, no comprehensible mechanism, no way to "fight" it. usually handled by containment rather than resolution.
### p class - predator/predatory
hunts, and hunts humans specifically or incidentally. the xenomorph end of the spectrum. rare, and rarely survivable to report on, which is why these are the most mythologised and least understood, most of what's "known" about class p anomalies is secondhand, embellished, or both.
### c class - cognitive
the most dangerous to categorise, because it acts on minds rather than bodies or machines. compulsions, false memories, groups that develop identical dreams. nobody agrees where this category ends and mundane madness begins. reports are unreliable by definition, since the reporter's own mind is the compromised instrument.
