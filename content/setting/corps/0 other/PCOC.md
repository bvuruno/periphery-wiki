# Periphery Corporate Oversight Commission
PCOC was built so the megacorps could know what was happening in the periphery. the megacorps *requested it* and [[k. holdings]] built out a data collection body first and a regulator second. every filing, inspection, and fine pcoc issues exists primarily to **generate a record**: who's operating where, what they're extracting, what they're finding, what's gone wrong.
given the periphery's absence of a monopoly on force, PCOC enforces its will through **its control of the connector nodes**:
- every company operating in the periphery answers to PCOC's reporting and licensing requirements
- a corp that doesn't comply **is banned from using the connector pads**, so they are stuck on the periphery or not able to access it
- on top of that, the non-compliant corp gets **embargoed by the megacorps**, inherently getting cut off from supply chains, contracts, and financing

the result is a regulator with almost no direct power and near-total effective compliance.
