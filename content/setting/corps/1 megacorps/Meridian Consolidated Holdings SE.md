## profile
a titan of infrastructure and logistics, meridian built its empire on shipping lanes, orbital tethers, and the boring machinery that keeps colonies breathing. they own the pipes, the roads, the power grids, the skeleton of civilisation. their logo, a stylised compass rose, is stamped on everything from water recyclers to funeral services.
