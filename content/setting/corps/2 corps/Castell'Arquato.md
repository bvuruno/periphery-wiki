# Castell'Arquato S.p.A.
**focus:** news, narratives, sports leagues, sensory deprivation tourism
## profile
old-money media dynasty that treats propaganda like opera. grand, self-serious, performed in a dead language nobody questions anymore. sells the rich a week of total sensory silence to "unplug" from a news cycle their own family writes.
## fun fact
their sensory-deprivation tourism division was originally built as a rehab program for their own burned-out anchors, before someone realised rich people would pay for it too.
