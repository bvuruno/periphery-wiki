# Hazemeyer Credit AG
**focus:** micro-lending, frontier banking, indenture financing
## profile
they lend to colonists the big banks won't touch, at rates that would make a loan shark blush. but they're the only game in town, and they collect. enforcers are polite, punctual, and armed with spreadsheets.
## fun fact
their debt collectors are trained never to raise their voice. company doctrine holds that a calm collector recovers more than an angry one, and the data backs it up.
