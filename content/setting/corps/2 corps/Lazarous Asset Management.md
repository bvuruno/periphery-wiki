# Lazarous Asset Management plc
**focus:** resource futures, commodity speculation, colonial debt instruments
## profile
ex-quants who went rogue, betting against the big players with stolen data. they win just often enough to stay paranoid.
## fun fact
the founding partners left their old firm after allegedly stealing proprietary trading data. nobody's proven it, but nobody at lazarous denies it either, and they've built a whole brand out of the ambiguity.
## rivalry
