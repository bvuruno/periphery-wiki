# Somnus Labs, LLP.
**focus:** energy, gravity labs
## profile
a nordic planets state research lab. somnus doesn't publish results, it publishes press photos, gleaming labs, gravity wells nobody outside can verify, and a habit of hiring away any researcher who gets close to something real. the secrecy reads as luxury, which is the point.
## fun fact
nobody outside the company has ever confirmed a working gravity well exists every "demonstration" happens behind closed doors, invitation only, no recordings allowed.
