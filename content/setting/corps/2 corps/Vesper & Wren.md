# Vesper & Wren LLP
**focus:** pmc (patrol, smuggling, piracy), beacon response, whistleblower extraction
## profile
founded by an ex-special-forces officer and an ex-ngo field coordinator who never fully stopped arguing about what the company is for. they will genuinely save your life. they will also genuinely bill you for it, and the whistleblower extraction line is both their most profitable service and the one most likely to get everyone killed.
## fun fact
the two founders still argue, in front of clients, about whether the company exists to protect people or to profit off protecting people, nobody's ever gotten a straight answer out of either of them.
