# Virla Resource & Cultivation Ltd.
**focus:** food production, terraforming, atmospheric engineering
## profile
family-run and proud of it. twenty two generations, one ledger, no outside shareholders. insures the colonies it terraforms, then insures the insurance. nothing on a virla world fails without someone already having been paid for the possibility.
## fun fact
the entire company answers, on paper, to one grandmother's holding trust. every cousin running a branch office still needs her sign-off for anything over a certain size.
