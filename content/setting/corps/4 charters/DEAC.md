# DEAC Deep Encroachment & Survey Charter
**focus:** buoy-laying and survey in unexplored/unstable space
## profile
the charter you join when you've got nothing left to lose, or you're chasing the hazard bonus hard enough not to think about the odds. highest mortality rate in the galaxy, worn like a badge. deac crews have their own gallows-humor culture, tattoos for systems they've mapped, memorial walls longer than crew rosters.
## fun fact
deac keeps a memorial wall at every waypoint depot, names, systems mapped, cause of loss where known. new recruits are shown it before they're shown anything else.
## rivalry
none, nobody else wants the work badly enough to compete for it.
