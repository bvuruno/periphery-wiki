# HVAC Hazard Valuation & Acquisition Charter
**focus:** scavenging, jump-claiming, first-tag salvage rights
## profile
pure vultures, and proud of it. the moment a distress beacon or a dead transponder hits the network, hvac crews are already burning fuel to get there first. claim law in the periphery runs on "first tag wins," and they've turned that into a religion. everyone hates them. everyone's called them at least once.
## fun fact
hvac crews maintain an unofficial, constantly-updated leaderboard of fastest claim-tags in the sector. pure bragging rights, no prize, and people have died chasing the top spot anyway.
## rivalry
[[SEAC]], over premature salvage; [[REAC]], over who "actually" found a wreck first when both show up on the same distress call.
