# MTAC Medical Triage & Auxiliary Care
**focus:** field trauma medicine, quarantine response, black-clinic supply runs
## profile
half of them are true believers who took the beltway posting because the old orbit wouldn't let them practice; the other half got struck off somewhere and this is the only place nobody checks the license. both halves save lives at the same rate, which tells you something about the licensing boards.
## fun fact
mtac field medics carry two kits. the official one, and the unofficial one stocked with whatever [[Pavel Medical]] can supply them with.
## rivalry
none formal, though [[SEAC]] crews grumble that mtac gets called in for the "easy" trauma work while they handle the toxic cleanup.
## relationship
closest thing to a standing partnership with [[Pavel Medical]], mtac field-tests half of what pavel can't get properly licensed, in exchange for supply at cost.
