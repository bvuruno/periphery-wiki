## old.orbit
**pop.** ~21 billion total
the core. old empires, federations, republics, even the odd socialist state, the lucky first-worlders who never had to leave the comfort of established law. home of the 5 megacorps.
## beltway
**pop.** ~4 billion
**est.** ~5000s
a looser stretch of corporate controlled planets and operations. authority thins into something to warlordism. corps own and battle for their share of the pie. 
## periphery
**pop.** ~3 million
**est.** ~6800s
the frontier. extraction minicorps, struggling settlements and auxiliary charters map and explore for the good and riches of mankind. the peripherys connector pads are controlled by [[PCOC|PCOC]] and divided into 6 subzones by their respective connector node, each node is too far away from each other to travel.

### communications
information travels by the same **launch pad system** as everything else, messages hops **from pad to pad in about 10 hours**.
- old.orbit --> beltway: news arrives the same day (20h)
- beltway --> periphery: about 5 days (110h)
- periphery --> outpost: 3-6h
some packets get lost in transit, some arrive corrupted. some stations forward everything they receive. others filter, delay, or sell access.
