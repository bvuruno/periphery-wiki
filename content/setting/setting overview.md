## the periphery field guide
deep in the granules of space, far away from empires and megacorps there is the **periphery**, the frontier of space exploration. minicorps inhabit this zone to mine and settle new planets and asteroids, and when things go wrong they call the **auxiliary charters** for help, small teams of people who risk their lives to do odd jobs in space.
youll join [[REAC]] the *reconnaissance and engineering auxiliary charter* **division C, farside**
### where are we?
it is the **eighth millennium** (8000). civilisation thins out the further you get from the center, the number of permanently inhabited worlds could be counted in the low hundreds:
- [[zones#old.orbit|the old.orbit]] sits at the core, dense with old empires, federations, and republics, the comfort of **established law** (~21b pop.)
- [[zones#beltway|the beltway]] lies beyond, a looser stretch of **corporate controlled planets and warlords**, where the rule of law starts to bend to whoever's paying (~4b pop.)
- [[zones#periphery|the periphery]] is past that, the frontier, it is divided into **6 subzones** with their respective connector pads. the closest thing we have to government here is the [[Periphery Corporate Oversight Commission]] **(PCOC)**, they control the connector pads and levy fines to unregistered operations (~3m pop.)
- [[zones#dim|the dim]] is the furthest you can get its dark, unmapped and unstable. almost completely void of life, **the true dark where help stops answering** (data not gathered)
### the pads?
interstellar travel has never been easy or fast. **ships depend on launch pads**, massive space stations that shoot entire ships across the void, **chained together to move across zones**. information travels by the same system as everything else. they are tremendously expensive to build and maintain, whole cities have grown up around the busiest pads. 
the beltway and the periphery are connected by 6 connector nodes, built one at the time, managed by PCOC.
- cn1 meridian's rest (~850k pop.)
- cn2 corvane landing (~700k pop.)
- cn3 greyharbor (~200k pop.)
- cn4 halyard reach (~500k pop.)
- cn5 tarpit junction (~400k pop.)
- **cn6 farside** (~250k pop.)

### do we get paid?
as a worker for a charter you get a monthly salary:
- marine 250cr/mo
- android 250cr/mo
- scientist 450cr/mo
- teamster 300cr/mo

jobs get posted in the OPERATIONS TERMINAL by color:
- **green.** regular jobs, minicorps *(1,000-4,000cr)*
- **yellow.** hazards included, minicorps *(4,000-8,000cr)*
- **red.** needs connections, corps *(8,000-15,000cr)*
- **black.** high-rep only, corps  *(15,000-30,000cr)*
- **gold.** megacorp job *(???)*

as a registered PCOC chater we are obligated to distress signals *(we are not obligated to take any work pro-bono but the charter maintains as an ESG principal to take ~50% of distress calls pro-bono)*
### what else is out there?
what we encounter and cannot explain we categorise and file as **"anomalies"**, it is common knowledge they exist but we are not sure of their nature or reason. the most accepted theory is that they are attracted to activity. 
we divide them into (from **least to most dangerous**):
- [[anomalies#s class|s class]] structural inconsistencies
- [[anomalies#t class|t class]] tech, unexplained inconsistencies and/or signal deterioration 
- [[anomalies#p class|p class]] predators, hunts humans or livestock 
- [[anomalies#c class|c class]] cognitive

**field procedure** encountering an anomaly follows:
1. do not engage
2. confirm it's actually reportable
3. identify the class
4. route through your crew manager
5. don't file if you're not sure it happened
reports to PCOC are mandatory, youll gain a small bonus per of the detail of the report.
### REAC?
its one of many charters in the periphery, their youngest crew manager ([[vinny kessler]]) and division (crew C), are stationed in farside. apart from jobs you can also seek out long term contracts with any corporations, explore farside sectors, investigate intercepted comms/rumors and catalogue anomalies
