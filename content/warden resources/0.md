### JOB POSTING
**SALVAGE - DATA RECOVERY // OUTPOST MARROW CUT**
**LOC:** *COLDMARROW BELT*
**RWD:** *2,000cr*
**PST:** *CORMACK MINERAL HOLDINGS*
**BRF:** Small outpost station struck by unknown cause. Client requires the vessel's black box recovered for insurance claim purposes. Station otherwise abandoned; salvage rights unclear but unlikely to be contested.

### what actually happened
- the ship's engineer was a shapeshifting anomaly, embedded in the crew undetected for some time.
- suspicion built among the crew that _someone_ wasn't human. paranoia, not confirmation.
- the engineer, cornered or exposed, shot the pilot and deliberately crashed the ship into/near the outpost rather than be caught.
- station evacuated in the chaos; nobody official knows _why_ yet, just that something violent happened and the ship went down.
- the engineer's current whereabouts still on-site.

### intro
introductions to the mission by vinny and key for the black box drawer

### the prac beat
after they launch near the station and dock, they receive a garbled transmission on one pc's comms confirms a prac ship has also launched near.
- prac's established rivalry
- creates urgency/pressure without adding a monster, the tension is "we need to move faster than a rival crew,"
- gives you a built-in reason for the split teams we need to move fast before PRAC arrives

### split-party structure
- **black box team**: needs to reach the wreck/bridge section, retrieve the box.
- **reactor/loot team**: checking if the reactor's salvageable for resale. vinny will tell them to look into this and/or loot anything left

### objectives completed
- **black box team**: they see that the pilot was shot from behind and the captains neck was bitten through and a leg is missing. the balck box drawer was scratched and beaten something was trying to get inside 
- **reactor/loot team**: going down the station they encounter a small tent and a suitcase with 3,000cr and a bunch of IDs (one from corvane minerals Tobin Ash) and a half eaten leg, the reactor is salvageable but its the older kind, it need 2 pcs or a hand cart

### prac shootout
shoots are fired in the other docking wing, PRAC is fighting something
