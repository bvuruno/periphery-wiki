## opener

hello and welcome to **the Periphery !!!**

### here’s what you need to know
- you decide what to do and where to go
- you form your own groups with a player cap of (#) players each session
- all session end in the ship
- not every fight is fair, running away is wise
- after each session, someone writes a report
- sharing is caring share maps, rumors, and discoveries with other players
- downtime actions help you prepare between adventures

### system consideration 
- **character death is common** 
  *(i will warn you when youre in danger of being one-shotted but be aware of the fragility of the pc)*
- **violence is punishing**
  *(violence is deadly and should be avoided at all costs, i wont design an encounter with a fight as the only option)*
- **the odds to be stacked against you**
  *(rolls are punishing, always try to find a creative way to do something and i will reward you)*
- **you'll make difficult choices**
  *(surviving, solving a mystery, or saving the day are often mutually exclusive)*

### how to have a good time
- *dont* force yourself to attend to every session
- *dont* force yourself to read every message or document, play when you want to avoid burnout
- *dont* be scared to post the first message, the west marches formula thrives on player communication and community

## faqs - WELCOME TO REAC *(New Crew Orientation)* 

### CHARACTER CREATION / CREW-JOINING RULES 
- **how does a new player create a crew member?** 
  if you have mothership experience there is a (google doc) in the bottom of the channel, add a new tab and write up crew member 
  if youre new to the system i have some premade sheets, dm me or say in the schedule channel that youre new so i can help you 10-15 minutes before the session 
- **if my character dies mid-session, can i make a new one right away, or do i sit out?**
  youll have to wait until the crew is out of danger and rejoin as someone else

### MONEY, GEAR & JOBS 
- **do we get paid mid-job or only on completion/return to pad?** 
  on return, job payout gets split into 50% to crew / 10% to ledger / 40% to the charter
- **what happens to payout if the crew fails the job or has to bail?** 
  if its a normal job, not much, someone else will take it 
  if its a contract, you wont get paid and the contractor will remember that 
- **what happens if we don't finish a job in one session, does it carry over, or does each session wrap cleanly?**
  when the time is up youll have to return to the ship, but you can return in later session to keep working on the job
- **can we buy/upgrade gear between sessions?** 
  yes, check the downtime channel is for this 
- **is there a death/injury payout or insurance, given how dangerous jobs are?** 
  small injuries will be covered by REAC, more severe ones, out of crew pocket
