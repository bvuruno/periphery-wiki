## the milky way
civilisation thins out the further you get from tbe centre.
- the *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#old.orbit\|old.orbit]]* is at the core, a *sector* of densely populated planets that have centralised governments. 
- the *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#inner beltway\|inner beltway]]* is after that, a looser stretch of corporate-controlled systems. 
- the *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#outer beltway\|outer beltway]]* is past that, **a dead zone spanning thousands of light-years**. barren of any star or planet, not even light could penetrate its veil. humanity thought that was the end of space. they were wrong.

long time ago, something possessed **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Meridian Co\|Meridian Co]]** to look into the *outer beltway* and they found a speck of light shining beyond the veil. naturally, they sent the *Corvane Expedition* with enough food and energy to last a century. 172 years later, confirmation came back. there was something beyond the *milky way*. **Meridian** coined the name *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#periphery\|periphery]]* and after a second expedition they discovered **it was rich in every resource humanity yearned for**. it also harboured *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.5 the known unknowns#anomalies\|anomalies]]* creeping in from the dark.

not long after the discovery, **Meridian** called a *megacorp* meeting through **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/k.holdings\|k.holdings]]**, the *megacorp* mediator, to propose the *Orion Accord*, **dividing the periphery into five zones**, one for each *megacorp*:

1. **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Meridian Co\|Meridian Co]] - [[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#corvane landing\|corvane landing]]**
2. **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Kovacs-Oyabu\|Kovacs-Oyabu]] - [[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#greyharbour\|greyharbour]]**
3. **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Mithras\|Mithras]] - [[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#halyard reach\|halyard reach]]**
4. **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Dept. 7\|Dept. 7]] - [[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#tarpit junction\|tarpit junction]]**
5. **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/TAIYANG H.I.\|TAIYANG H.I.]] - [[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#farside\|farside]]**

![milky way.png](/img/user/the%20periphery/images/milky%20way.png)

**Meridian**, with its pioneer knowledge of *launchpads*, started construction of the first *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#connector nodes\|connector node]]*, a series of four *launchpads* stitched together to cross the *periphery* in a matter of days. **Meridian** tried **operating its zone directly**, but learned the hard way that **it was too costly**: too many hazard payouts, too many *anomalies* disrupting operations, too many leeches stealing company secrets. 

after a century struggling, **k.holdings** pitched the *Oversight Program* to the *megacorps*. the plan was to morph the *periphery* into a contractor economy, reducing *megacorp* exposure. 
then, **k.holdings** would establish a [[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/PCOC\|commission]] **(PCOC)** to **foster a highly competitive market**. meanwhile the *megacorps* could hold back and tax everything coming in and out **of** their *connector nodes*.
another bonus of this scheme was the **massive quantities of data harvested by PCOC**. every mineral, every *anomaly*, every person of interest is stored in databases shared with the *megacorps*.

> [!info] tldr 
> - the *periphery* is far away from the populated centre
> - it is divided into five *zones*
> - the *zones* are too far apart to travel between
> - getting there requires a *connector node* controlled by the *megacorps*
> - **getting back is never guaranteed**


## the present day
over the centuries, the periphery has become the **prime exporter of rare resources**. companies could extract some of the same resources from the *old.orbit* and *the beltway*, **but not in the same quantities without doing irreparable damage to their own worlds**. 
over time the *periphery* has become the only *zone* with the know-how to extract and refine these resources on the industrial scale that the old.orbit demands.
a curious phenomenon is noted: the more extraction takes place, the more frequent *anomaly* sightings become. **in some cases previously quiet beltway zones now report anomaly sightings**.

today, the *periphery* is a **booming economic centre**. contractors from all over *the beltway*, even specialised professionals from the *old.orbit*, venture to the *periphery* for work. 
most never stay permanently. it's a temporary, deadly, lucrative job for the greediest souls in the galaxy. 
the most desperate people dream of working in the _periphery_ **through an indenture contract or a one-way ticket** and pray to hit it big one day.

the *megacorps* don't directly operate in the periphery any more; they let the tier-twos *(mesos)* and tier-threes *(micros)* do the extracting and rudimentary refinement. 
from there, the resources get transported to *the inner beltway* where they are manufactured into low-value goods or shipped to the old.orbit where the *megacorp* factories are.
**the only requirement is that they follow PCOC's licence agreement**; if they don't, they are banned from using the *connector nodes* and embargoed by the *megacorps*.

a new type of corporation has been created in the *periphery* by **PCOC**. the *charters* are quick response teams that **function as the emergency services in the periphery**.

> [!info] tldr 
> - the *periphery* is **rich in rare resources**
> - the other *sectors* depend on its resource extraction
> - extraction makes *anomaly* sightings more frequent
> - the *periphery* is an escape from poverty so it attracts loads of low-skill labour
> - the *megacorps* don't operate directly in the *periphery*. they let the *mesos* and *micros* do the heavy work
> - PCOC employs the *charters* to run basic services
