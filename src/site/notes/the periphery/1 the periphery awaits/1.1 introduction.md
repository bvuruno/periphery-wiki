# what is a Periphery campaign
a *Periphery* campaign is an **OSR**-inspired playstyle made to be played as a **westmarches** and/or **open-table**.
- **OSR.** a player-driven playstyle focused on:
	- *rulings over rules.* GMs make quick, logical decisions instead of consulting complex rules.
	- *player skill over character skill.* success depends on clever player problem-solving instead of stats in a character sheet.
	- *unbalanced worlds.* encounters are not scaled to a specific level, characters are fragile and combat is deadly; which encourages stealth, negotiation, retreat, etc.
	- *recommended reading.* 
		- [Principia Apocrypha](https://lithyscaphe.blogspot.com/p/principia-apocrypha.html)
		- [What is DnD's OSR style of play? - Questing Best (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b_krKx8NOhM)
		- [TTRPGs: What the heck is the OSR? - Joseph R. Lewis (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N5U5rznzWho)

- **westmarches.** a type of open table focused on player-driven exploration, a roaster of rotating players and a large wilderness map.
	- *no regular schedule.* sessions are scheduled by the players whenever they are available rather than meeting on a fixed night.
	- *no regular party.* different combinations of players form unique groups from a larger roster for each adventure.
	- *no regular plot.* there is no pre-written overarching story; the narrative is shaped entirely by where the players choose to explore.
	- *recommended reading.*
		- [Grand Experiments: West Marches - ars ludi (blog)](https://arsludi.lamemage.com/index.php/78/grand-experiments-west-marches/)
		- [Meet the Campaign: Intro to West Marches - Cannibal Halfling Gaming (blog)](https://cannibalhalflinggaming.com/2021/10/27/meet-the-campaign-intro-to-west-marches/)

- **open-table.** a campaign format that allows a rotating roster of players to drop in and out of self-contained sessions
	- *recommended reading.*
		- [Open Table Manifesto - The Alexandrian (blog)](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/38643/roleplaying-games/open-table-manifesto)
		- [Open Table: How the Creators of D&D Ran Their Games - The Arcane Library (blog)](https://www.thearcanelibrary.com/blogs/shadowdark-blog/open-table-how-the-creators-of-d-d-ran-their-games)
