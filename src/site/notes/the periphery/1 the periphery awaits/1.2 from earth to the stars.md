## timeline
### late 3000s
- humanity settled its first planet.
### late 4000s
- **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Meridian Co\|Meridian Co]]** pioneered the *launchpad*.
### mid 6000s
- after constant tensions with the empires and federations of the *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#old.orbit\|old.orbit]]*, the then-four *megacorps* established the *[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#beltway\|beltway]] sector*.
### mid 7000s
- **Meridian** sent the *Corvane Expedition* to explore the outer beltway. 172 years later, they received a single message from the void: **"novus orbis"**.
- **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/TAIYANG H.I.\|TAIYANG H.I.]]** became the fifth *megacorp*.
### 8010
- **Meridian** called for the *Orion Accord*, dividing up the *periphery* into five *zones*.
### 8131
- **Meridian** constructed the first *connector node* and tried to assert direct control of its *zone*: **[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#corvane landing\|corvane landing]]**
### 8278
- **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/k.holdings\|k.holdings]]** called a *megacorp* meeting to propose the *Oversight Program*, distancing the *megacorps* from the *periphery* by creating **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/PCOC\|PCOC]]** *(the periphery corporate oversight commission)*.
### 8368
- the second connector node finished construction: **[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#greyharbour\|greyharbour]] ([[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Kovacs-Oyabu\|Kovacs-Oyabu]])**.
### 8579
- the third connector node finished construction: **[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#halyard reach\|halyard reach]] ([[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Mithras\|Mithras]])**.
### 8766
- the fourth connector node finished construction: **[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#tarpit junction\|tarpit junction]] ([[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/Dept. 7\|Dept. 7]])**.
### 8888
- the fifth connector node finished construction: **[[the periphery/1 the periphery awaits/1.3 places to be#farside\|farside]] ([[the periphery/2 the corps/2.1 megacorps/TAIYANG H.I.\|TAIYANG H.I.]])**.
### 9123
- present day
