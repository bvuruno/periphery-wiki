# sectors
a *sector* is a rough portion of the galaxy. there is no real marker where a *sector* starts and stops. this leads to constant tension between the *old.orbit* and *the beltway* over where jurisdiction begins and ends.

![milky way.png](/img/user/the%20periphery/images/milky%20way.png)

## old.orbit
it sits at the core of the galaxy, dense with old empires, federations, republics, even the odd socialist state. the lucky first-worlders who never had to leave the comfort of established law. the number of permanently inhabited worlds is in the mid hundreds. 

even though the *megacorps* fought tooth and nail for the old empires to recognise the beltway, they never moved their headquarters there. to this day the five *megacorps* have old.orbit addresses.
## beltway
after the beltway was established, *mesos* flocked to it to carve out their own markets, not to be crushed under the stranglehold the *megacorps* had in the *old.orbit*.
### inner beltway
the habitable part of the beltway, home of *mesos*, caudillos and cheap factories.
### outer beltway
the dark veil that surrounds the galaxy.
## periphery
the frontier, divided into five zones, each with its own _megacorp_ owner. **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/PCOC\|PCOC]]** acts as the regulator. unlike the other *sectors*, the *periphery* does not have a stable population; it varies a lot depending on what is discovered. # space travel
## launchpads
interstellar travel was never easy or fast. **ships depend on launchpads**, massive space stations that shoot entire ships across *sectors*. they are tremendously expensive to build and maintain; whole cities have grown up around the busiest pads. 

travel within and between the *old.orbit* and *the beltway* is simple enough, just like a metro station or a bus stop. within the *old.orbit*, **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/k.holdings\|k.holdings]]** established a neutral transport authority, the **[[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/UTA\|UTA]]**, to avoid conflict between the *megacorps*. in the beltway, *megacorps* and *mesos* operate mixed pads.
## connector nodes
travelling to the *periphery* is more complicated. *the beltway* and the *periphery* are connected by *connector nodes*, four *launchpads* **chained together** to cover the distance needed to reach each other. at the ends there are *connector stations*; most of the *periphery* population lives around them.
## communications
information travels by the same **launchpad system** as everything else. messages hop **from pad to pad, about 8-10 hours each**.
- *old.orbit --> beltway*: news arrives in 24h
- *beltway --> periphery*: about 3-4 days
- *local periphery --> outpost*: 3-6h
some packets get lost in transit; some arrive corrupted. some stations forward everything they receive. others filter, delay, or sell access.
