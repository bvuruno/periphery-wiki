# DIVERSIFIED CONGLOMERATES (*megacorps*)
what makes a corp ***mega***? well it all boils down to scale. all the *megacorps* have survived long enough to hoard a critical mass of companies and subsidiaries that prevent them from going bankrupt. somewhere in this mess there are industries that mint credits.

all *megacorps* have these key industries, but each one has carved out its own edge:
1. high-end manufacturing & heavy industry
2. resource extraction & refinement
3. financial services
4. energy generation & gravity labs
5. colonial development & terraforming
6. security & internal control
7. expeditionary forces & asset recovery
8. media & communications
9. cognition department (ai lab)
10. biotechnology & synthetic integration (androids)
11. transportation, shipyards & orbital yards (they all take turns making launchpads for the [[the periphery/2 the corps/2.0 regulatory bodies/UTA\|UTA]])

# auxiliary charters
the *auxiliary charters* work as service contractors for **PCOC**. they are the replacement of social services like emergency services, police, firefighters, etc.

| charter  | full name                                          | profile                                                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ARAC** | *arbitration & resolution auxiliary charter*       | mediating mesos/micros disputes before they escalate to violence                      |
| **AXAC** | *anomaly exposure auxiliary charter*               | first-response quarantine to an anomaly incident, before it spreads or gets exploited |
| **DEAC** | *deep encroachment auxiliary charter*              | buoy-laying and survey work in unstable/uncharted space                               |
| **EVAC** | *emergency vacate auxiliary charter*               | mass evacuation of a failing station/colony before structural collapse                |
| **LOAC** | *logistics & operations auxiliary charter*         | emergency resupply, cargo escort, supply-chain triage for boom-towns                  |
| **PRAC** | *protective response auxiliary charter*            | escort, detainment, riot response, VIP/cargo protection                               |
| **REAC** | *reconnaissance and engineering auxiliary charter* | recon, engineering odd-jobs, general fixer work                                       |
| **RTAC** | *relay & transmission auxiliary charter*           | comms relay repair, courier runs, restoring contact after a blackout                  |
| **SEAC** | *structural evaluation & auxiliary charter*        | deep-space demolition, hazmat/biochem containment, station-scrubbing                  |
| **TEAC** | *trauma & emergency auxiliary charter*             | trauma stabilisation, casualty extraction, mass-casualty triage                       |
