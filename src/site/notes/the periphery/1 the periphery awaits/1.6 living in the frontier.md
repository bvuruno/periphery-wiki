## do you have the time?
time after the first human colonies has been regulated as atomic earth time (AET), which runs as a 24 hour clock, 12 months a year, 30 days a month, with a 5-day week appended at the end for celebrations. it is divorced from actual earth time. it functions as a standard in all sectors.
