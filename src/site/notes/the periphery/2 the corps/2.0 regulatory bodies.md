# Periphery Corporate Oversight Commission
**PCOC** was built by **k.holdings** as part of the *Oversight Program*, its role in the *periphery* is to act as a market overseer and data harvesting program.

**PCOC** enforces its will through its control of the *connector nodes*:
- every company operating in the *periphery* answers to **PCOC** and agrees to **PCOC's** license requirements.
- a corp that doesn't comply **is banned from using the connector pads**, so they are stuck on the *periphery* or not able to access it.
- on top of that, the non-compliant corp gets **embargoed by the megacorps**, inherently getting cut off from supply chains, contracts, and financing.

the result is a regulator with almost no direct power and near-total effective compliance.
