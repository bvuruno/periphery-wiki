# Meridian Consolidated Holdings SE
a titan of infrastructure and manufacturing. **Meridian** built its empire on *launchpads* and chips. the boring technology that keeps the galaxy turning. it own the pipes, roads, power grids, the skeleton of civilisation. their logo, a compass rose, is stamped on everything from water recyclers to funeral services.
## relationships
- **Kovacs-Oyabu**
- **Mithras**
- **TAIYANG H.I.**
- **Dept.7**
# Kovacs-Oyabu GmbH & Co. KG
a frankenstein merger of heavy industry and biotech, **Kovacs-Oyabu** operates with two faces. the grimy foundries and forge-worlds of the **Kovacs** line, and the sterile white labs of the **Oyabu** division. the corporate culture is famously bifurcated; engineers wear black, researchers wear white and never the twain shall they meet except at the quarterly bloodlettings they call board meetings.
## relationships
- **Meridian Co**

- **(!) Mithras**
	**K.O** prides itself on the weapons it builds; **Mithras** gets rich melting them down or reselling them, tainting **K.O's** name.

- **(x) TAIYANG H.I.**
	the old guard sneers at **Taiyang's** "stakeholder ecology" branding; **Taiyang**, in turn, undercuts **K.O** on every terraforming contract with cheaper, greener pitches.

- **(?) Dept.7**
	**Dept.7** has quietly acquired more than one bankrupt **K.O** subsidiary over the centuries; nobody at **K.O** likes to be reminded which ones.

### tier 2

# Mithras Foundries LLC
specialists in salvage, reclamation and "asset recovery". **Mithras** engineers are notable for walking into radiation-soaked wrecks and walking out with functional reactor cores. they are pioneers in relay stations and reactors. their communication logistics arm runs a good chunk of the relay network that ferries news between zones, which means **Mithras** often knows a disaster happened before anyone receives the emergency beacon.
## relationships
- **Meridian Co**

- **(x) Kovacs-Oyabu** 
	**K.O** stopped suing **Mithras** and started engineering against them instead. self-destruction protocols built into their weapons lines specifically tuned to recognise and defeat **Mithras's** own recovery operations. it's an insult. the galaxy's best salvage outfit treated as a pest rather than a rival worth acknowledging.

- **(!) TAIYANG H.I.**
	failed terraforming projects are some of **Mithras's** best salvage contracts, but **Taiyang** refuses to sell any failing colony to them.

- **(?)** **Dept.7**
	they're locked in a constant battle over who offers the lowest-latency trading.

### tier 2
- **($) EPICAC Cryptographic Services**
	mostly contracted to break the other *megacorps'* encryption.
# TAIYANG HEAVY INDUSTRIES (太阳重工)
the newest of the *megacorps*, **Taiyang Heavy Industries** burst onto the scene a millennium ago with a proprietary atmospheric seeding process that cut terraforming timelines by 40%. founded by a consortium of former colonial administrators and agritech visionaries. **Taiyang** sells worlds, or at least the promise of them. it builds domes, it cracks ice, it turns dead rock into farmland. the old *megacorps* sneer at their optimism, their green branding, their insistence on "stakeholder ecology".

## relationships

- **(x) Mithras**
	**Mithras** profits when **Taiyang** fails. more than once, a struggling colony's collapse has come suspiciously fast right before **Mithras** arrives with a salvage contract and saviour narrative already forming. nothing's ever been proven, just a pattern **Taiyang's** board has stopped calling coincidence.

- **(?) Dept.7**

### tier 2
- **($) Antikythera Systems**
	frequently hired by **Taiyang** to reverse-engineer competitors' terraforming tech.

- **(!) Ilium Habitat Solutions**
	market rivalry. **Taiyang** sells the beautiful terraformed future, **Ilium** sells the ugly, functional now and colonists keep choosing **Ilium**.
# Department 7, Pty LLC
it appear after disasters, wars, and corporate collapses. always in identical grey suits, always with paperwork already drawn up. it acquire distressed assets, failed colonies, and "compromised biological holdings" for pennies on the credits and flip them to the *mesos*.
