# Kovacs-Oyabu GmbH & Co. KG
a frankenstein merger of heavy industry and biotech, **Kovacs-Oyabu** operates with two faces. the grimy foundries and forge-worlds of the **Kovacs** line, and the sterile white labs of the **Oyabu** division. the corporate culture is famously bifurcated; engineers wear black, researchers wear white and never the twain shall they meet except at the quarterly bloodlettings they call board meetings.
